Company profile

Công ty TNHH quốc tế Minh Huy is a Vietnamese company specializing in the production and export of OE (open-end) yarn. With sustainable development, product quality and business reputation as its priorities, Minh Huy continues to invest in production and strengthen its position in the international OE yarn market.

View factory photos

Production capacity

Our production capacity is approximately 1,800 tonnes of yarn per month. Our modern facilities, machinery and equipment support large production volumes, consistent quality and customers’ delivery requirements.

We produce OE yarn in counts from Ne 21 to Ne 40 for a variety of applications, especially weaving and knitting.

Product quality

With “Quality Is Our Number One Priority” as our motto, Minh Huy places product quality and production consistency first.

We maintain strict quality control throughout the process, from raw material selection and spinning to quality inspection and final packaging. Our goal is to deliver consistent quality that meets technical standards and each customer’s requirements.

Export markets

Currently, 100% of Minh Huy’s products are exported to international markets, including China, South Korea, India, Pakistan and other countries.

This reflects our production capabilities, the quality of our products and the trust international customers and business partners place in Minh Huy.

Development and partnerships

With our production capacity, diverse product range, consistent quality and export experience, Minh Huy aims to build long-term, sustainable and mutually beneficial partnerships.

We are committed to improving product quality, upgrading production technology and strengthening our supply capabilities to become a trusted OE yarn manufacturer and supplier for customers worldwide.
